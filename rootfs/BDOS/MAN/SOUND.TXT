SOUND - where the sound goes

SYNOPSIS
     SOUND
     SOUND MUTE | UNMUTE | VOLUME 0-100
     PLAY file.wav

DESCRIPTION
     Every session has its own sound: the machine's console is one, and
     each bterm session is another. SOUND says where this session's goes,
     its volume, and what's playing in it.

     The console's sound goes to the machine's sound card (Intel HDA, as
     most PCs have and QEMU's -device intel-hda gives). With none - a
     server, a VM with no sound card - it goes nowhere, and that's fine.

     A bterm session's sound plays on the bterm computer, whether or not
     this machine has a sound card: a DOS game's Sound Blaster, its FM
     music and PC speaker, PLAY, and native programs. bterm -q doesn't ask
     for it, and CONTROL sound.remote=0 keeps every bterm session silent.

     MUTE and VOLUME are this session's alone.

     DOS programs see a Sound Blaster 16 at port 220h, IRQ 7, DMA 1 and 5
     (their BLASTER variable says so), an OPL2 at 388h, and the PC
     speaker, whatever the machine really has.

EXAMPLES
     SOUND
     SOUND VOLUME 40
     PLAY C:\BDOS\SOUNDS\CHIMES.WAV
