CTTY - move this shell to another console

SYNOPSIS
     CTTY [COM1 | CON | \\HOST]

DESCRIPTION
     CTTY on its own says which console this shell uses.

     CTTY COM1 moves it to the first serial port (AUX is the same), and
     CTTY CON brings it back to the screen and keyboard.

     CTTY \\HOST makes this console a terminal on another bdos machine:
     you get a prompt there, with this machine's current directory as its
     T:, until you EXIT.

EXAMPLES
     CTTY COM1
     CTTY \\OFFICE
