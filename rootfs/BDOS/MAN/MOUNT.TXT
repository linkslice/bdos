MOUNT - CD images as drives

SYNOPSIS
     MOUNT
     MOUNT d: image.iso[,image2.iso...]
     MOUNT d:
     MOUNT d: /D

DESCRIPTION
     MOUNT d: image.iso makes drive d: a CD drive with that ISO 9660 image
     in it. Long names (Joliet) work, as do the 8.3 names DOS programs use.
     A CD drive is read-only. The image can be anywhere bdos can read: C:,
     T: (on the bterm computer), a NET USE drive.

     Give several images, with commas between them, and the drive has a
     changer with all of them in it: the first is in the drive, and DISC
     (or Ctrl+Alt+D in a game) changes it. That's for games on more than
     one CD.

     MOUNT on its own lists the CD drives and their discs; MOUNT d: shows
     one. MOUNT d: /D takes the drive away.

     DOS programs find the drives the way they find real ones, through
     MSCDEX (it's built in), so games that insist on a CD are happy.

     A MOUNT in a bterm session belongs to that session, like its T:. The
     machine's own MOUNTs are everyone's.

EXAMPLES
     MOUNT D: C:\GAMES\DOOM2.ISO
     MOUNT E: T:\CD1.ISO,T:\CD2.ISO,T:\CD3.ISO
     MOUNT D: /D
