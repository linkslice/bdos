CONTROL - settings, and the Control Panel

SYNOPSIS
     CONTROL [LIST]
     CONTROL key=value
     CONTROL applet                 (on the desktop)

DESCRIPTION
     bdos keeps its settings in C:\BDOS\SETTINGS.INI, a key=value a line.
     CONTROL lists them all with a line about each; CONTROL key=value
     changes one. Changes take effect at once and are saved.

     On the desktop, CONTROL opens the Control Panel, and CONTROL applet
     opens one applet: DATE, NETWORK, DESKTOP, THEMES, MOUSE, KEYBOARD,
     DOS, SOUND, DISCS (the Disc Changer) or SYSTEM. CONTROL LIST lists
     the settings there too.

   Some to know
     clock.tz, clock.dst, net.ntp   the clock (MAN AFTERBOOT)
     net.ip, net.mask, net.gateway  a fixed address (default: dhcp)
     net.password                   what bterm must give
     net.udp                        0: no bterm at all
     net.cpu                        the machine CPU uses (MAN CPU)
     dos.speed                      xt, at, 386, 486, pentium or max
     dos.scale                      sharp, smooth or stretch
     sound.remote                   0: bterm sessions are silent
     desktop.theme                  Hot Dog Stand, for one

EXAMPLES
     CONTROL dos.speed=386
     CONTROL desktop.theme=Hot Dog Stand
