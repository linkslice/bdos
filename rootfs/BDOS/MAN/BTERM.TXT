BTERM - this machine from another computer

SYNOPSIS
     bterm [options] host[:port]          (on Linux or macOS)
     bterm -l [host]

DESCRIPTION
     bterm is a separate program for Linux and macOS, from
     github.com/linkslice/bterm (make, with SDL2 for the windows). It
     connects to a bdos machine and shows its desktop in a window, its
     screen (-t), or its prompt in the terminal (-c). The folder bterm was
     started in becomes T: on bdos for that session, and the session's
     sound plays on the bterm computer.

     Each bterm gets a session of its own: its own T:, its own CD drives,
     its own sound. The machine's own screen carries on as it was.

OPTIONS
     -t          the machine's screen: C:\> prompt, DOS games full screen,
                 WIN for the desktop; EXIT closes it
     -c          a bdos prompt in this terminal (Ctrl-] hangs up)
     -d a.iso[,b.iso...]
                 a CD drive with these discs in its changer, at the first
                 free letter from D:. Another -d is another drive. On bdos,
                 DISC or Ctrl+Alt+D changes the disc. The first disc is in
                 when the session starts.
     -g WxH      the desktop size (default 1024x768; with -t, 640x480)
     -s dir      share dir as T: (default: the current directory)
     -n          share nothing as T:
     -p password the machine's net.password ($BTERM_PASSWORD works too)
     -z N        scale the window N times
     -f          keep the desktop's size; resizing only scales it
     -q          quiet: don't ask for the session's sound
     -l [host]   list the bdos machines on the network
     -v          log what T: is asked for, and the link's numbers

     The port is 17979 unless you say otherwise. Under QEMU, forward it:
     -nic user,model=e1000,hostfwd=udp:127.0.0.1:17979-:17979

EXAMPLES
     bterm office                      the desktop of \\OFFICE
     bterm -t -d doom2.iso office      its screen, with D: a CD
     bterm -c -d d1.iso,d2.iso,d3.iso -d tools.iso 192.168.1.50
                                       D: has three discs, E: one

ON THE BDOS SIDE
     CONTROL net.password=word         bterm must give it
     CONTROL net.udp=0                 no bterm at all
     CONTROL sound.remote=0            bterm sessions are silent

     There's no encryption: use bterm on networks you trust.
