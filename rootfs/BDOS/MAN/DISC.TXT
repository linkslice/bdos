DISC - change the disc in a CD drive

SYNOPSIS
     DISC
     DISC d:
     DISC d: n | NEXT | EJECT
     DISC d: ADD image.iso

DESCRIPTION
     A CD drive (from MOUNT, or bterm -d) has a changer: a list of discs,
     one of them in the drive. DISC lists every CD drive and its discs; =>
     marks the one that's in, with its volume label.

     DISC d: n puts disc n in (counting from 1). NEXT puts the next one in,
     going round. EJECT leaves the drive empty: programs reading it get
     "not ready", as with a real drive. ADD puts another image file in the
     changer of a MOUNTed drive (bterm's discs are bterm's: give it another
     -d).

   In a game
     Ctrl+Alt+D, in any DOS program, pauses it and shows the changer over
     its screen. Up and down choose a disc, Enter puts it in, E ejects, Esc
     goes back without changing anything. A line at the top of the screen
     says what's in for a few seconds afterwards. Programs that write lines
     rather than draw a screen get a line to answer instead.

     The game sees the change as a real one: MSCDEX reports the media
     changed, as games check before asking for disc 2.

   On the desktop
     Ctrl+Alt+D opens the Disc Changer (also in the Control Panel and in
     Accessories), which does all of this with buttons.

EXAMPLES
     DISC                 what's in where?
     DISC D: 2            the second disc
     DISC D: NEXT
     DISC D: ADD C:\GAMES\CD4.ISO
