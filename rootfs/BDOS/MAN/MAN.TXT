MAN - the manual

SYNOPSIS
     MAN
     MAN page
     MAN /K word

DESCRIPTION
     MAN on its own lists the pages, with a line about each. MAN page
     shows one, a screenful at a time: any key for more, Q or Esc to stop.
     MAN /K word lists the pages that mention a word.

     MAN AFTERBOOT is the place to start.

     The pages are text files in C:\BDOS\MAN: TYPE and EDIT work on them,
     and a NAME.TXT you put there is MAN NAME.

     HELP lists every command with a line each; HELP name shows one.

SEE ALSO
     HELP, MAN AFTERBOOT
