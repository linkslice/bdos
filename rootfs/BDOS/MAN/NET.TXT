NET - the bdos machines on the network

SYNOPSIS
     NET
     NET VIEW
     NET USE [x: \\HOST\C\DIR]
     NET USE x: /D

DESCRIPTION
     bdos machines find each other on the local network by name, with no
     setting up. HOSTNAME shows or sets this one's name.

     NET says what this machine is called and its adapter. NET VIEW lists
     the bdos machines that answer. NET USE x: \\HOST\C\DIR makes drive x:
     a folder on another machine; NET USE lists those drives, and
     NET USE x: /D lets one go.

     Across a router, or from a PC, bterm is the way in (MAN BTERM). TCP/IP
     is there too: IPCONFIG, PING, TELNET, FETCH, NSLOOKUP, NTP.

     CONTROL net.password=word makes other machines and bterm give the
     password. There's no encryption, so keep it to networks you trust.

EXAMPLES
     NET VIEW
     NET USE G: \\OFFICE\C\GAMES
